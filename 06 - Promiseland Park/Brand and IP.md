# Brand and IP

## Brand
PromiseLand Park.

## Working Tagline
"Where Hope Leads the Way" — CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED.

## Brand Feeling
1950s family warmth + timeless fantasy + biblical storytelling + modern premium theme park design.

## Originality Constraint (OFFICIAL)
Do not copy any existing theme park's:
- Characters
- Logos
- Fonts
- Ride stories
- Architecture
- Music
- Catchphrases
- Attraction names

## IP Checklist (for future trademark/copyright review)
- [ ] Trademark search: "PromiseLand Park"
- [ ] Trademark search: each land name (Hope Street, Promise Plaza, Garden of Grace, Faith Harbor, Glow Haven, Promise Frontier)
- [ ] Trademark search: each attraction name (Where Are You, Peace Be Still, Journey of Grace, Garden of Light, Glow Parade, Beyond Tomorrow)
- [ ] Trademark search: tagline "Where Hope Leads the Way"
- [ ] Copyright/originality review of any finalized story, music, or character designs before production
- [ ] Attorney engagement for formal filing

**No trademark should be treated as available until professionally searched.**

## Tagline Candidates Surfaced from Concept Art (2026-08-24) — CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED

Land-specific taglines appeared as on-image text in the second batch of concept visuals (see [[Park Master Plan]]) — not the result of a deliberate copywriting pass, but worth capturing as real creative candidates rather than discarding just because of how they surfaced:

- **"Where He calls your name"** — [[Garden of Grace]]. Directly echoes the land's own core story beat ("Where are you?" → grace) — the strongest of the three.
- "Your journey begins here" — [[Hope Street]].
- "The heart of PromiseLand" — [[Promise Plaza]].

None of these replace or compete with the park-level working tagline ("Where Hope Leads the Way") — they're land-level candidates, a different tier of copy, equally unapproved.

## Founder Vision cross-reference (2026-08-24)
[[Founder Vision]] sets the marketing standard directly: PromiseLand doesn't hide being Christian, but marketing's primary message must never be "this park is not for you" — the intended feeling is "this is the story we believe, come experience it with us, you are welcome here." Reviewed the current working tagline ("Where Hope Leads the Way") and the land-specific candidates (including "Where He calls your name" for [[Garden of Grace]]) against this: **no contradiction found** — all read as invitational, not exclusionary. This standard should govern all future copy and tagline decisions, not just what already exists.
