# Hope Street 🛍️

The welcoming entrance avenue of [[Promiseland Park]] (see [[Park Master Plan]]). Leads directly into [[Promise Plaza]].

## Theme
"Old stories meet new dreams." An old-world marketplace atmosphere combined with a modern family shopping district.

## Potential Experiences (CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED)
- Bakery
- Coffee house
- Family restaurant
- Marketplace
- PromiseLand merchandise
- Artisan shops
- Street musicians
- Storytellers
- Fountains
- Landscaping
- Seasonal decorations

## Sensory Experience
Sensory design is important here: guests might smell fresh bread and coffee, hear music and fountains, and see families gathering together.

## Spatial Plan (2026-08-24) — COMPACT PREMIUM CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED
Reconciled acreage: **~9 ac** (range 8–10), within the full allocation in [[Park Master Plan]]. Dense premium entrance district — shared-wall buildings, real occupied ground floors, theatrical/forced-perspective upper stories, courtyards, alleys, landscaping, music, fountains, and scent all doing the work of making a compact street feel rich and alive. Buildings share back-of-house, kitchens, and storage where practical, feeding into the single backstage spine described in [[Park Master Plan]] — guests perceive many individual places even though they operate efficiently together behind the scenes.

## Status
