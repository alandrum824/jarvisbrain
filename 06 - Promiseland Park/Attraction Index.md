# Attraction Index

Catalog of PromiseLand Park's headline attractions, organized by land. Each attraction has its own note with a standard set of sections; anything unknown is marked TBD — Requires Professional Validation rather than guessed.

## By Land

### Garden of Grace
- [[Where Are You]] — primary attraction, immersive boat experience.

### Faith Harbor
- [[Peace Be Still]] — first major attraction, immersive boat/motion.
- [[Journey of Grace]] — second major attraction, large-scale immersive walkthrough.

- [[Garden of Light]] — daytime interactive gardens.

## Balanced Phase 1 Attraction Phasing (2026-08-24)

All **RECOMMENDATION — NOT YET APPROVED**. Not yet synced into each attraction note's own "Phase" field (still shows TBD there pending Adam's approval) — this table is the working recommendation.

| Attraction | Land | Phasing | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| [[Where Are You]] | Garden of Grace | **OPEN DAY 1** | Moderate technical complexity (boat dark ride, not a mega-scale build), strong all-ages family appeal, essential to establishing the park's emotional/brand identity immediately. |
| [[Peace Be Still]] | Faith Harbor | **OPEN DAY 1** | Similar scale/complexity to Where Are You; needed to give Faith Harbor a real Day 1 anchor and deliver one of the park's core emotional beats early. |
| [[Journey of Grace]] | Faith Harbor | **PHASE 2** | Explicitly described as intended to become one of PromiseLand's largest immersive attractions — highest technical complexity (living first-century town, staged capture sequence, sensitive story content) and highest cost intensity of anything in the park. The sensitivity of its content (the crucifixion-adjacent finale) also argues for more design iteration time, not a rushed opening-day build. |
| [[Garden of Light]] | Glow Haven | **OPEN DAY 1** | Lower complexity and cost intensity than a major dark ride, high family appeal, gives Glow Haven a daytime purpose from the start. |
| [[Glow Parade]] | Glow Haven | **PHASE 1B** | High technical complexity and cost intensity (floats, projection, choreographed performers, dedicated route infrastructure) — too much for Day 1, but important enough to the park's day/night balance that it's targeted as the first major post-opening addition rather than deferred to Phase 2. A lighter nightly lighting moment covers Glow Haven after dark until then (see [[Park Master Plan]]). |
| [[Beyond Tomorrow]] | Promise Frontier | **PHASE 1B, realistically may slip to PHASE 2** | Story is explicitly not finalized — operational difficulty and cost intensity are unknown until it's locked. Phase 1B is the aspirational target if the story resolves in time; Phase 2 is the honest fallback since nothing can be built against an undecided story. |

**Net Day 1 lineup: 3 headline attractions** (one each in Garden of Grace, Faith Harbor, Glow Haven), consistent with how comparable real regional/family parks open — see the acreage-benchmark comparables in [[California Site Search]].

**Cost research (2026-08-24):** order-of-magnitude ranges for all three Day 1 attractions, plus three costed implementation options for [[Peace Be Still]] (physical water ride vs. motion simulator vs. hybrid — a real, meaningful cost swing between them), now exist in [[Financial Model]]. None of the three options is selected — this is cost information to inform the choice, not a decision.

**Spatial placement (2026-08-24):** all three Day-1 attractions now have a conceptual location in [[Park Master Plan]]'s first real spatial master plan — each show building sits at the rear of its land against the shared backstage spine, hidden behind themed landscaping/streetscape rather than presenting as a visible structure. [[Peace Be Still]]'s working implementation is currently **HYBRID — NOT YET APPROVED** (physical boarding/launch + simulator storm sequence + experiential finale), evaluated as the strongest premium-per-dollar option. Expansion pads are conceptually reserved (not designed) for [[Journey of Grace]], [[Beyond Tomorrow]], and one additional attraction each in Garden of Grace and Glow Haven.

### Supporting attractions — Phase 1 (all CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED)
A theme park can't run on headline rides alone. Recommended lower-cost supporting experiences to help Phase 1 feel complete:
- A gentle family-friendly play/exploration area — **CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED**
- A short walkthrough/discovery trail in Garden of Grace as a pre-show environmental-storytelling complement to [[Where Are You]] — **CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED**
- A live street-entertainment program (musicians, storytellers) along Hope Street, tying directly to that land's own theming — **CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED**
- A small interactive family water-play area (splash-pad style) in Glow Haven or Garden of Grace — **CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED**
- A live family stage show, either in Promise Plaza or a small dedicated venue — **CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED**
- A gentle, high-throughput family ride (carousel-style) centrally located near Hope Street or Promise Plaza — **CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED**
