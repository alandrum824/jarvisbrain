# Glow Haven ✨

One of the four lands branching from [[Promise Plaza]] in [[Promiseland Park]].

## Theme
Joy, light, and celebration.

## Daytime Attraction
[[Garden of Light]] — interactive gardens using color, water, music, light, and family interaction.

## Nighttime Feature
Glow Haven dramatically transforms after sunset. [[Glow Parade]] — an illuminated celebration involving floats, performers, music, projection, light, and color. Intended to be one of PromiseLand's strongest nighttime environments.

## Spatial Plan (2026-08-24) — COMPACT PREMIUM CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED
Reconciled acreage: **~12 ac** (range 10–14), including [[Garden of Light]]'s footprint (nested, not additive) — see [[Park Master Plan]] for the full allocation. One physical environment engineered to do two jobs: the same landscaping, water, pathways, architecture, and lighting infrastructure serve [[Garden of Light]] by day (gardens, water, interactive elements, family exploration, color) and transform dramatically by night (lighting, projection, music, water, interactive effects) toward the eventual [[Glow Parade]] — maximizing investment efficiency without looking cheap. Pathways and lighting infrastructure are reserved to eventually support the full parade; **Glow Parade is not assumed to open Day 1.**

## Status
**OFFICIAL:** Glow Haven is an official land; the theme and the daytime/nighttime attraction concepts above (see [[Decision Log]]).
Attraction technical/operational detail is tracked separately in [[Garden of Light]] and [[Glow Parade]] and is largely TBD — Requires Professional Validation. The spatial plan above is COMPACT PREMIUM CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED.
