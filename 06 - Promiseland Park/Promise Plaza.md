# Promise Plaza 🏛️

The central hub of [[Promiseland Park]]. Guests arrive here from [[Hope Street]]; all four lands branch from here: [[Garden of Grace]], [[Faith Harbor]], [[Glow Haven]], [[Promise Frontier]].

## Working Phrase
"Every path begins here."

## Centerpiece (CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED)
Currently envisioned around a major original fountain/water feature rather than a castle. Not finalized — see [[Open Questions]].

## Spatial Plan (2026-08-24) — COMPACT PREMIUM CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED
Reconciled acreage: **~6 ac** (range 5–7), deliberately kept compact so it feels alive rather than oversized — see [[Park Master Plan]] for the full allocation. Provides the guest's first major reveal after Hope Street: the fountain/water centerpiece (icon still not finalized), landscaping, shade, seating, entertainment capability, strong night lighting, and wayfinding, with controlled sightlines toward all four lands so guests see hints of each without seeing the whole park at once.

## Status
**OFFICIAL:** Promise Plaza is the central hub connecting all four lands (see [[Decision Log]]).
