# PromiseLand Park — Project Brain

The permanent Home / Map of Content (MOC) for the PromiseLand Park project. Every PromiseLand note links back here or through here.

## Vision

A future California family theme park centered on Faith, Hope, Grace, Love, Family, Kindness, Forgiveness, Courage, Imagination, Joy, and Purpose. See [[Founder Vision]] for the underlying faith, service, and hospitality philosophy that guides design, staffing, and marketing.

The park combines the warmth and optimism of classic mid-century family entertainment with modern immersive theme park design. It should feel magical, nostalgic, wholesome, welcoming, and family-first — with an original identity, not a copy of any existing entertainment company's IP.

**Founding goal — affordability:** a world-class family experience should not feel out of reach for ordinary families. No ticket price has been established.
