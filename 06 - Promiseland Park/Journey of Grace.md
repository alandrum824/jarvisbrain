# Journey of Grace

Second major attraction of [[Faith Harbor]] in [[Promiseland Park]] — intended to become one of PromiseLand's largest immersive attractions.

## Land
[[Faith Harbor]]

CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED (story and theme are OFFICIAL per [[Decision Log]]; ride/operational design below is not).

## Core Message
Following Jesus does not make us better than other people. It calls us to love them. This is the single attraction most directly anchored by [[Founder Vision]]'s "faith without superiority" principle — reviewed 2026-08-24, no contradiction found, but flagged as the part of the park most at risk of drifting into "us vs. them" storytelling if the Roman-occupation/capture sequence below isn't executed carefully. The Samaritan-inclusion beat and the existing no-graphic-violence, no-excessive-intensity constraints already align with that standard; hold future scene-level design to it explicitly.

## Ride Type
Large-scale immersive walkthrough (living first-century town).

## Guest Emotional Arc
Curiosity/community → tension (occupation, capture) → grief → darkness → freedom and grace.

## Scene-by-Scene Experience
Guests move through a living first-century town and encounter people from different communities, including Samaritans. The experience progresses into Roman occupation and a controlled theatrical capture. Guests should experience tension without physical restraint or excessive violence. The story moves toward Jesus's sacrifice.

**Explicit constraint: do NOT depict the crucifixion graphically.** Use silhouette, environment, lighting, sound, and music instead. Full scene-level staging is TBD — Requires Professional Validation.

## Finale
Moves from darkness into freedom and grace. Staging detail TBD — Requires Professional Validation.
