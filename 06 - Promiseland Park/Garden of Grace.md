# Garden of Grace 🌿

One of the four lands branching from [[Promise Plaza]] in [[Promiseland Park]].

## Theme
Creation → beauty → hiding → forgiveness → grace.

## Primary Attraction
[[Where Are You]] — a peaceful immersive boat experience beginning in an Eden-inspired environment.

### Story Progression
The Beginning → Wonder → Something Changes → Hiding → "Where are you?" → Grace → Home.

God is not physically depicted; His presence is represented through light, sound, and atmosphere.

### Core Message
You don't have to hide from God. You can come to Him. Grace is waiting.

## Spatial Plan (2026-08-24) — COMPACT PREMIUM CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED
Reconciled acreage: **~15 ac** (range 13–17), including [[Where Are You]]'s show building/ride footprint (nested, not additive) — see [[Park Master Plan]] for the full allocation. Creates scale through dense mature landscaping, winding pathways, waterways, waterfalls, rockwork, berms, elevation changes, and hidden sightlines rather than raw acreage — guests should rarely be able to see the land's actual boundaries. The attraction's show building sits at the rear of the land against the backstage spine, disappearing behind landscape and themed scenery rather than presenting as a visible structure. A concealed expansion pad is reserved for a second Garden of Grace attraction (not designed yet).
