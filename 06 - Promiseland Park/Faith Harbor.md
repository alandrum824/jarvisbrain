# Faith Harbor ⚓

One of the four lands branching from [[Promise Plaza]] in [[Promiseland Park]].

## Theme
Faith when life becomes uncertain.

## First Major Attraction
[[Peace Be Still]] — an immersive boat/motion attraction.

### Story
Harbor → Boarding → Calm Water → Storm → Fear → "Peace. Be still." → Complete calm → Walk toward Jesus.

### Core Message
You were never alone in the storm.

## Second Major Attraction
[[Journey of Grace]] — intended to become one of PromiseLand's largest immersive attractions.

Guests move through a living first-century town and encounter people from different communities, including Samaritans.

### Core Message
Following Jesus does not make us better than other people. It calls us to love them.

### Story Notes
The experience progresses into Roman occupation and a controlled theatrical capture. Guests should experience tension without physical restraint or excessive violence. The story moves toward Jesus's sacrifice — the crucifixion is **NOT** to be depicted graphically; use silhouette, environment, lighting, sound, and music instead. The finale moves from darkness into freedom and grace.

## Spatial Plan (2026-08-24) — COMPACT PREMIUM CONCEPT — NOT YET APPROVED
Reconciled acreage: **~20 ac** (range 17–23) — the largest single land and one of the park's strongest visual environments, including [[Peace Be Still]]'s show building and the harbor itself (nested, not additive) — see [[Park Master Plan]] for the full allocation. A dense, original waterfront village (not copied from any existing themed land) built around a strategically sized harbor (~4–6 ac of actual water, part of the park-wide ~5–12 ac water plan) made to read as much larger through curved shoreline, buildings obscuring the water's edge, bridges, islands, rockwork, and boats. Narrow streets, alleys, courtyards, market stalls, upper-story facades, and forced perspective build the "much larger world" illusion. [[Peace Be Still]]'s current working implementation is **HYBRID — NOT YET APPROVED**: physical boarding/initial movement, an immersive simulator/show storm sequence, and an experiential finale — evaluated in [[Financial Model]] as the strongest premium-per-dollar option, not a final selection. A concealed expansion pad is reserved for [[Journey of Grace]] in a later phase — not designed in this pass.
