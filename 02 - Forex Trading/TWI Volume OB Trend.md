## Rules (all inputs adjustable)
- **Structure (v6):** swing = highest/lowest of 5 candles each side (pivot, confirmed 5 bars late). A candle CLOSE past the last unbroken swing: same direction as structure = **BOS** (thin dashed line); against it = **CHoCH** (solid, flips structure); a CHoCH whose candle body ≥ 1 ATR = **MSS** (thick). Last 8 kept. The same rule runs on H1/H4/D (last closed candle) for the table's Structure column; the table header shows the chart's own structure. Alerts: BOS, CHoCH, MSS.
- **Key zones (v6, range swings 15 since v9):** dealing range = last 15-candle swing low ↔ swing high; dotted EQ 50% line; above = PREMIUM (sells), below = DISCOUNT (buys). BUY ZONE = closest demand at/below price among base zones (strong get +0.25 ATR edge), bullish OBs and PDL; SELL ZONE = mirror (supply bases, bearish OBs, PDH). Outlined box.
- **Panel (v11):** one table, bottom left. Header = HTF alignment + chart structure; rows H1/H4/D; then the trade map rows.
- **Trade map rows (v6):** Bias = H4 + D EMA trend AND structure all agree (else Mixed); Location; Buy/Sell zone (range, type, distance in ATR); Verdict = LOOK FOR BUYS (bull bias + discount + zone) / LOOK FOR SELLS (bear bias + premium + zone) / WAIT + reason. A map, not a signal; not backtested.
- **Base zones (v4):** a run of 3–12 candles whose total high–low range is ≤ 1.5 ATR, then one push candle with a body ≥ 1.5 ATR that closes outside the run. The longest qualifying run is used. Zone = the run's full high to low, dashed border; green = pushed up (demand), red = pushed down (supply). Label "BASE 2.1 ATR ×2" = push size and the number of times price came back into the zone and held. "STRONG BASE" + thick border when the zone sits within 0.5 ATR of PDH/PDL or a live volume OB. Deleted once a candle closes through it; 6 kept per side. Alert: "Price entered a base zone".
- **Daily levels (v3):** yesterday's high/low as solid lines (PDH blue, PDL purple) and today's high/low so far as dashed lines, labeled at the right edge. The day = TradingView's daily candle for the symbol (OANDA gold rolls at 5 PM New York). Intraday charts only.
- **HTF rows (v3, in the panel since v11):** H1 / H4 / D rows. Trend = EMA 21 vs 50 on that timeframe. Strength = ADX(14): Strong ≥25, Building 20–25, Weak <20. Momentum = MACD histogram growing in the trend's direction ("Pushing") or shrinking ("Fading", orange), plus RSI(14). Reads the last CLOSED candle of each timeframe (no repaint; it only changes when that candle closes). Rows at or below the chart's timeframe show "—". Bottom row: alignment (e.g. 3/3 DOWN or Mixed). Alert: "HTF alignment changed".
- **Trend:** EMA 21 > EMA 50 = uptrend → green candles; otherwise red. EMAs hidden by default.
- **High-volume order block:** the last opposite-colored candle before ONE strong candle whose body ≥ 1.0 ATR(14), that closes beyond the OB candle's high (bull) / low (bear), on volume ≥ 1.5× the 20-bar average (tick volume on forex). Box = the OB candle's full range, labeled with the volume multiple ("OB 2.1x vol"). Extends right while live; deleted once a candle closes through it. Max 8 per side.
- **Losing steam:** trend still on, but the MACD(12,26,9) histogram has shrunk 3 bars in a row (above zero in an uptrend, below zero in a downtrend). Orange ✕ "fading", once per fade.
- Closed bars only (no repaint). Alerts: bullish OB, bearish OB, trend up, trend down, losing steam.
