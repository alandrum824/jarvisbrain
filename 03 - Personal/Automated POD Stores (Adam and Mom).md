# Automated POD Stores (Adam and Mom)

A new venture, separate from [[Mom's eBay Store (EMarketingTX)]] (which is just about clearing her existing clothing inventory to free up house space). This is about building **two separate print-on-demand stores** — one for Adam, one for his mom — each with no overhead: no monthly platform fees, no physical inventory, no hands-on fulfillment.

## Structure
- **Two distinct stores**, not a shared one. Adam runs his own; mom runs her own. Confirmed 2026-08-21.
- Print-on-demand model: supplier (e.g. Printful, Printify) prints and ships per order only when it sells — nothing held, nothing paid until a sale happens.
- Proposed build: storefront on **Base44** (Adam already builds on this — see [[Base44 Apps]] — pay-as-you-go, no subscription) wired to a POD supplier's API for fulfillment, Stripe/PayPal for payment.

## Main tradeoff flagged
POD margins are thinner than bulk resale. The real work isn't the automation — it's the niche and designs each store sells. Automation solves the overhead problem, not the "will people buy this" problem.
