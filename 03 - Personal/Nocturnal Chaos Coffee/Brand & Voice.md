# Brand & Voice

Part of [[Nocturnal Chaos Coffee]]. Source: Adam's briefing, 2026-09-29.

- **Personality:** "Coffee for real mornings, busy families, road trips, practices, workdays, and whatever chaos comes next."
- **Voice:** warm, premium but fun, a little sarcastic, built for real life. Not corporate, not coffee-snob, not gothic horror.
  > "No fake tasting notes. No coffee-snob attitude. Just good coffee for busy mornings and the people you share them with."
- **Product tagline:** "Your cup before the chaos."
- **Optional creative line (don't force it everywhere):** "The first cup gets us talking. The second gets us in trouble."
- **Look:** the owl logo; **black, deep purple, and silver only. No gold.** No occult symbols, and no moons or symbols on the owl's forehead. Logo file: `03 - Personal/Nocturnal Chaos Coffee - owl logo.png`.
- **Soft call to action:** "Save Me a Cup" / waitlist. **No "shop now" until launch.**
- **No Western/cowboy customer theme.**

Earlier brand history (approved supporting lines, the faith line) lives in [[O For the Love of Coffee]]. The tagline order conflicts with it: see [[Overview & Status]], conflict 3.
