## 1. Refund / Return Policy

**The short version**
We want you to get great coffee. Because coffee is a food product, we do not accept returns of coffee, and we don't ask you to mail coffee back if something goes wrong. Returned coffee can't be resold.

**If something's wrong**
Email oh4theloveofcoffee24@gmail.com with your order number and what happened. Photos help for damaged shipments.
1. **Order, listing or address mistake:** We'll help fix it. Replacement costs may apply depending on the situation.
2. **Fulfillment error** (wrong grind, wrong label, etc.): We'll make it right with a replacement at no charge to you.
3. **Carrier damage, theft after delivery, or "delivered" but missing:** We'll review it case by case. We may ask you to check nearby, wait 24 hours, or contact the carrier. Replacements, when offered, never require mailing product back.

**How replacements work**
If we replace an order, we send a new shipment and share tracking when it's moving. You won't need to return the original bag. Refunds, if issued, go back to the original payment method.

**Address accuracy**
Please double-check your shipping address and product selection before ordering. Once an order is in fulfillment, changes may not be possible.

## 2. Shipping Policy

**Free standard shipping to all 50 U.S. states and DC. APO/FPO addresses and PO boxes are supported. No minimum order.**

**Where we ship**
- All 50 states and DC: free standard shipping
- APO/FPO addresses and PO boxes: supported
- We currently ship only to the destinations above. Other destinations are not available at this time.

**Who prepares your order**
Orders are prepared and shipped by our roasting partner, Temecula Coffee Roasters, in Temecula, California.

**Timing (estimates, not guarantees)**
- Order preparation and shipping: about 2–5 business days after you place your order
- Delivery: about 1–5 business days after your order ships

Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding holidays. Holidays, weather and carrier delays can add time. Expedited shipping is not available.

**Carriers and tracking**
Orders ship via USPS, UPS or FedEx, whichever works best for the package. You'll get tracking by email when your order ships. Tracking may show "pre-shipment" until the carrier scans the package, and that's normal.

**Address accuracy**
Please double-check your shipping address at checkout. Once an order is in fulfillment, it generally can't be changed. Contact us right away if you spot an error.

**Questions or delays?** Email oh4theloveofcoffee24@gmail.com with your order number.
