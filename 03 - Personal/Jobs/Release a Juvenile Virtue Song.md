# Release a Juvenile Virtue Song

**The job:** Help Adam through his actual release workflow for Juvenile Virtue (Christian rap / alternative rock) — Suno → DistroKid → platforms → TikTok promo — with three concrete responsibilities, confirmed by Adam 2026-08-18.

## Boot chain (read these, in order, and you have the skill)
1. This note, end to end.
2. [[Juvenile Virtue]] — the artist identity, platforms (Spotify, Apple Music, jvsession.com, TikTok @juvenilevirtue), and DistroKid as the distributor.
3. [[Grow Juvenile Virtue on TikTok]] — the growth strategy and the evidence behind it. Read before drafting any TikTok copy.

## The flow
1. **Suno** — AI-assisted songwriting/production.
2. **DistroKid** — distribution to streaming platforms.
3. **Platforms** — Spotify, Apple Music, etc. go live.
4. **TikTok promo** — @juvenilevirtue.

## What Jarvis actually does at each stage

**1. Songwriting/Suno side**
Help draft lyrics or Suno prompts before a track gets made. This is creative collaboration, not just admin — Adam leads on direction and voice, Jarvis helps develop it.

**2–3. Release tracking**
Keep a running log of what's released, what's pending on DistroKid, and when it went live on each platform, so nothing gets forgotten between releases. Log entries in this note under "Release Log" below — add a new one per song, don't let it go stale.

**4. TikTok promo**
When a song drops, draft TikTok caption/post copy in Adam's voice, ready for him to review and post himself — never auto-post anything, this is draft-only per the standing rule on sending anything publicly.

**Default to story format, not announcement format.** Verified against @juvenilevirtue's real numbers (2026-08-18): the two best-performing posts on the account (66.8K and 60.3K views, 2,346 and 2,865 likes) are both personal/narrative story content — an anniversary tribute, an AI-generated "youth group to forever" love story — neither is a direct "new song, go listen" pitch. The one direct-promo post checked ("I just launched the JV Room, listen to the music") got 117 views and 7 likes. That's roughly a 300x engagement gap. Don't draft announcement copy by default — draft a story the song rides along with. See [[Grow Juvenile Virtue on TikTok]] for the full strategy this feeds into.
