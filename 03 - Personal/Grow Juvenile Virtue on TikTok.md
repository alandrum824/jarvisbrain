# Grow Juvenile Virtue on TikTok

**The goal, stated by Adam 2026-08-18:** push @juvenilevirtue and actually grow a following — not just post when a song drops.

## Starting point (2026-08-18)
- 2,989 followers, 19.6K total likes, 2,006 following. The following-count is worth watching — close to the follower count, worth confirming later whether that's organic or follow-for-follow growth, since that affects how real the audience actually is.
- Reviewed the video grid directly (public, no login needed). Full findings and the two example posts are in [[Release a Juvenile Virtue Song]]'s TikTok-promo section — the short version: **story-driven personal/faith narrative posts wildly outperform direct music-promo posts** (~300x engagement gap between the two: 66.8K/60.3K-view story posts vs. a 117-view "go listen to my music" post).

## Strategy, based on that evidence
1. **Lead with story, not with the release.** The song is the soundtrack to a real story (faith journey, family, struggle-to-redemption), not the subject of the post.
2. **The two proven formats:** (a) real personal moments/photos with a reflective caption, (b) AI-generated narrative visuals telling a story arc, both using Adam's own voice as "original sound." Both are viable; don't assume only one works — there's only one data point each so far.
3. **Direct promo posts should be rare, not default** — the evidence so far says they don't move people. If one goes out, keep it short and let it ride on a moment/story rather than being the whole post.

## Open questions / not yet done
- Only reviewed 3 of the account's videos in depth (the two top performers + one low performer). A fuller pass through the rest of the grid would sharpen the pattern — worth doing before locking in the strategy as final.
- No posting cadence decided yet (how often, on what kind of story beats).
- Whether the 2,006-following count reflects real engagement or follow-for-follow growth — unconfirmed, worth checking who's actually in that following list at some point.
- Adam hasn't said whether he wants Jarvis proactively pitching story ideas between releases (not just at release time), or only engaging when a song is actually dropping.
